Shell renderer

Serve the repository root (python tools/serve.py --site .) and open /site/dev/shell.html. Add ?ghost=id for another ghost than the site's first. Drag characters, click, double-click, wheel; the log shows the MouseInfo stream. The quick buttons are the usual surface ids (0 to 8 the main character's, 10 and 11 the partner's, -1 hides the character); a ghost's other surfaces go in the surface box.
