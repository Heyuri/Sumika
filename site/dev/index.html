Sumika test page

This page includes one script in its <head>. Once the site has a ghost (docs/guide.md "Adding a ghost"), the site's first ghost appears in the bottom-right corner; until then nothing of Sumika shows, and the browser's console says that the site has no ghosts.

Go to the second page: the ghost should come back without greeting you again.

Add ?sumika-debug to the address to see the runtime's log in the console.

The other development pages of this folder: elsewhere.html (a page on another address for the storage hub; docs/guide.md "Pages on other sites"), and runtime.html, shell.html, balloon.html and sakura.html, which work only with the project root served (python tools/serve.py --site ., then /site/dev/).
